Documentation

Quote Calculator for Divi 5 adds quote forms to WordPress and a Quote Calculator module to the Divi 5 builder. Visitors answer a few steps, see an estimate, and send their contact details; you receive the request by e-mail and in the admin. This guide covers installation, every setting and the hooks available to developers.

Requirements

SoftwareVersion
WordPress6.5 or later (tested up to 7.1)
DiviDivi 5, theme or Divi Builder plugin
PHP7.4 or later

Installation

  1. In WordPress, go to Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin and upload d5-quote-calculator.zip.
  2. Activate the plugin. A Quotes menu appears, with an example form, "Bathroom renovation".
  3. Edit the example or create your own form, then add the module to a page (see Add the module).

The example form is created once. To skip it, add add_filter( 'd5qc_import_sample', '__return_false' ); before activating the plugin, or simply delete it.

Create a quote form

Go to Quotes > Add form. Give the form a title, then build it in the Quote form box: settings at the top, then the steps. Changes are saved with the Publish or Update button. If something prevents the form from working, the page lists it after saving, and the form is not shown to visitors until it is fixed.

Steps and questions

A form has up to 10 steps, each with a title, an optional description and up to 20 questions. Visitors see one step at a time, with a progress bar and Previous / Next buttons. Each question has a label, an optional help text and an "Answer required" switch.

Question typeShown to visitorsSettings
Single choiceClickable cards, one can be selected.Up to 30 answers, each with a price, an optional icon (emoji) or image.
Multiple choiceThe same cards, several can be selected.Same as single choice.
QuantityA number field with a unit, for example m².Minimum, maximum, step (1, or 0.5 for halves), default value, unit.
Short textA one-line text field.Placeholder.
Contact detailsName, e-mail, phone, postcode and message fields.Each field can be shown or hidden, and required or optional. The e-mail is always required.

Every form needs one "Contact details" question, usually in the last step: it collects the e-mail address of the request. The consent box and the send button appear on the last step.

Prices

Each answer of a choice question has a price, always entered before tax:

PriceExampleAdded to the estimate
No priceTiming: "Within 3 months"Nothing.
Fixed amount"Walk-in shower", 1 900 €The amount. A negative amount is a discount.
Unit price × quantity"Premium finish", 75 € multiplied by "Bathroom area"The unit price times the value of the chosen quantity question.

The estimate is the base price plus the prices of the selected answers, then VAT. Answers of hidden questions do not count. The price shown in the browser is indicative: when a request arrives, the server checks every answer and computes the total again from the form settings, and that total is the one stored and e-mailed.

Conditions

Any step after the first, and any question, can have a Show only if condition: pick an earlier single or multiple choice question, then the answers that show the item. In the example form, the "Equipment" step only appears for a full or partial renovation. Hidden questions are never required, and the progress bar counts only the steps the visitor will see.

Add the module

  1. Open a page in the Divi 5 Visual Builder.
  2. Add a module and choose Quote Calculator.
  3. In Content > Quote Form, choose the form. Turn on Show Form Title to display its title above it.

The builder shows a preview of the first step. The module also works in Divi Theme Builder layouts.

Quote requests

Requests are listed under Quotes > Quote requests, with the name, e-mail, phone and estimate. Open one to see the answers, the contact details, the estimate, the form, the date of consent and the date of automatic deletion. Requests cannot be edited; delete them with Move to Trash.

The professional e-mail is sent to the address set in the form, with the answers, the contact details, the estimate and a link to the request; its Reply-To is the visitor, so replying answers them. When the acknowledgement is on, the visitor receives the form title, their chosen answers, the quantities and the estimate, but none of the text they typed. E-mails are sent with wp_mail(): if your site uses an SMTP plugin, they go through it.

Privacy (GDPR)

  • Consent: visitors must tick a consent box to send a request. It links to the privacy policy page set in Settings > Privacy. The date of consent is stored with the request.
  • Retention: each form sets how long its requests are kept. A daily task deletes expired requests.
  • Export and erasure: requests are included in Tools > Export Personal Data and Tools > Erase Personal Data, found by the e-mail address.
  • Policy text: a suggested paragraph appears in Settings > Privacy > Policy Guide.
  • No external service, no tracking, no cookie. IP addresses are only used, hashed, to limit the number of requests.

Security

Requests are sent to a REST endpoint and pass several checks before anything is saved: a token signed with a secret of your site, a hidden field that only bots fill in, a minimum time between display and sending, and a limit of five requests per IP address every ten minutes. Every answer must be one the form offers; numbers must respect their bounds and step; contact details are validated. The browser never sends prices.

Accessibility

  • Answer cards are real radio buttons and checkboxes: Tab reaches them, arrow keys and Space select them.
  • When a step changes, focus moves to its title.
  • Errors are written next to their field, linked to it, and announced by screen readers.
  • The progress bar exposes the current step to assistive technologies; the estimate is announced when it changes.
  • Motion is reduced for visitors who ask their system for it.

JavaScript

The d5qc:sent event fires on the module after a request is sent. window.d5qc.init() starts quote forms added to the page later.

REST endpoint

POST /wp-json/d5qc/v1/submit receives the form ID, the signed token, the answers and the consent. It answers with a confirmation message and the estimate, or with the errors of each field.

FAQ

Does it work with Divi 4?

No. The module only exists for Divi 5.

Is the estimate a binding price?

No. It is shown as indicative on the page and in the e-mails. You confirm the final price in your own quote.

Can I compute a price per square metre?

Yes: add a Quantity question (for example the area in m²), then give an answer the price type "Unit price × quantity" and choose that question.

Why is my form not shown?

A form needs at least one step and a "Contact details" question, and it must be published. The form edit screen lists what is missing; logged-in editors also see the reason on the page.

Can I use it in several languages?

The texts of the plugin are translation ready, with English and French included. The questions and answers are your own content: write them in the language of your site.

The e-mails do not arrive.

The plugin uses the standard WordPress mail function. If your host does not deliver e-mails reliably, install an SMTP plugin; requests are always saved in the admin anyway.
