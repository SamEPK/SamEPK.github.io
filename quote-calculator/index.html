Quote Calculator
for Divi 5

A multi-step quote simulator for tradespeople and service providers. Visitors answer a few questions, watch the estimate update, and send you a detailed request.

Try the live demoRead the docs

Coming soon to the Divi Marketplace. Unlimited websites, one year of updates and support.

Quotes, not forms

Not a general form builder: one job, done simply. Steps, questions with prices, and a contact step.

Native Divi 5 module

Built on the Divi 5 module API. No compatibility mode, so the Divi 4 framework is never loaded and pages stay fast.

Live estimate

The price updates as visitors answer: fixed amounts, prices per m² or per unit, a base price, VAT, and an optional range.

Steps that adapt

Show a question or a whole step only when an earlier answer calls for it, so visitors never see irrelevant options.

Clickable answer cards

Single or multiple choice cards with an icon or an image, plus quantities, short text and contact details.

Requests by e-mail

You receive every request with the full summary; replying answers the visitor. An optional acknowledgement can go to the visitor.

Requests in WordPress

Every request is also stored in the admin, with the answers, the contact details, the estimate and the consent date.

GDPR ready

Mandatory consent, automatic deletion after the period you choose, and the WordPress tools to export or erase personal data.

Secure by design

Prices are never trusted from the browser: the server checks every answer and computes the total again. Bots are filtered out.

Full design control

Style steps, progress bar, cards, selected cards, fields, buttons and the estimate from the Divi 5 Design tab.

Build the form, style it in Divi

Quote forms are edited in the WordPress admin. The Divi 5 module picks a form and gives you every design option.

Every request, in your inbox

A clear summary of the project and the contact details. Reply to the e-mail to answer the visitor.

Requirements

Works with the Divi theme or the Divi Builder plugin.
